# Reconciliation - document-synthese-v1 vs PRD

## Scope

Comparison of `docs/document-synthese-v1.md` (source) with `prd.md` (PRD), 2026-08-24. Findings below are substantive requirements that are absent, materially narrowed, or contradicted. The PRD already covers the principal catalogue, order, distribution, preparation, AMAP, history, and responsive-use requirements.

## Findings

| Priority | Status | Source requirement | PRD treatment | Reconciliation needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Contradicted | Odoo is a commercial/fiscal back office while the application remains operational source of truth; for V1, a late transmission of final delivered-order data to Odoo is preferred (source ss.42.1, 42.3, lines 1332-1357 and 1387-1414). The integration must be isolated behind a connector and an Odoo outage must not block operations; failures must be recorded and retryable (source ss.42.9-42.10, lines 1555-1607). | Odoo integration, synchronization, and invoicing are explicitly excluded from V1 (PRD s.4, line 64); synchronization is only an evolution (s.10, line 210). NFR-007 preserves external-tool independence (line 187) but not the source's connector, retry, or delivery handoff. | Make an explicit product decision: either retain the PRD exclusion and mark source ss.42.1-42.10 deferred, or include a minimal asynchronous delivered-order export/synchronization with mapping, failure log, retry, and no operational dependency. |
| P0 | Missing / narrowed | Notifications include: publication notices, customer notice when an order is ready, AMAP reminder before the modification deadline, and administrator notice for a new order. The grower can configure preferences to avoid notification overload (source s.34, lines 958-988). | The PRD specifies only publication emails and their consent (FR-012--FR-015, lines 98-101), plus an in-app home-page signal for new orders (FR-048, line 143). It has no requirement to notify a customer that an order is ready, remind an AMAP member of a deadline, or manage grower notification preferences. | Add notification events, intended recipients/channels, delivery and preference rules. Preserve consent requirements for marketing/publication email while separately defining transactional messages. |
| P1 | Missing | The administrator manages clients (source s.4.1, lines 65-80); a classic customer is an operational contact associated with one or more orders (s.4.2, lines 84-100). The application must retain the contact data needed for operations, even when the person is not immediately created in Odoo (s.42.4, lines 1418-1433). | The PRD collects customer details during checkout (FR-017, line 106) and restricts access to them (FR-075, line 176), but does not require an admin contact/customer record, order history by contact, or contact reuse/management. | Add a minimal admin customer/contact capability: view the contact and its associated orders, correct operational details with traceability, and avoid requiring an account or Odoo record for a classic customer. |
| P1 | Weakened | An AMAP subscription includes both a default pickup day and a default pickup point (source s.23.1, lines 699-713). | FR-056 keeps only a singular `retrait par defaut` (PRD line 155), which does not unambiguously preserve the separate default day and point. | State separate fields for default pickup weekday/date rule and default pickup point, or explicitly define `retrait par defaut` as containing both. |
| P2 | Missing | Payment is not online; the chosen payment method is operational information and payment occurs when the order is collected (source s.13, lines 431-445). | The PRD records the planned payment method in checkout (FR-017, line 106) and excludes payment tracking (s.4, line 58), but does not specify that settlement occurs at collection. | Add the collection-time payment rule, or explicitly declare timing outside the product's responsibility so implementers do not introduce online/pre-delivery settlement behaviour. |
| P2 | Missing | A tour is a manually ordered route of villages/points, with no automatic optimization (source s.17, lines 514-533). | FR-043 requires ordered villages/points and approximate times (PRD line 135), and s.4 excludes automatic optimization (line 63), but does not explicitly say the administrator defines and maintains that order manually. | Clarify that route order is administrator-maintained, not merely displayed or system-derived. |

## Non-findings

- The PRD correctly retains manual availability management and the absence of automatic stock reservation/deduction (source ss.5-6; PRD FR-003--FR-007).
- Publication snapshots, classic and AMAP order lifecycles, historical commercial data, reports, dated market/tour occurrences, closure, AMAP suspension/cession/consumption, and mobile-first operational views are materially represented in the PRD.
